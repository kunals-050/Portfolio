01 / About

Economics gives data
a deeper context.

I’m drawn to the questions behind the numbers not just what changed, but why it may have changed and what to do next.

How I got here

My academic background spans mathematics and economics. Mathematics shaped how I approach problems; economics made me curious about markets, incentives, consumer behaviour and the choices people make with limited resources.

That curiosity led me to data analytics. I enjoy using Excel, SQL and Power BI to organise information, investigate business questions and communicate findings clearly. I bring an economics lens to the work: a change in sales, for example, is not just a number it may reflect price, demand, product mix, incentives or wider market conditions.
